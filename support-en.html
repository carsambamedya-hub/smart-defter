Frequently asked questions

  • Where is my data? Only on your phone. We can't see it.
  • What if I change phones? On the old phone, make a backup with Settings › “Full backup (with photos and documents)” and save the file to iCloud Drive, Google Drive or your computer; on the new phone use Settings › “Restore from backup”.
  • What if I delete the app? All data on the phone is deleted. Make a backup first.
  • I bought it — do I pay again on a new phone? No. Just tap “Restore purchase” with the same Apple / Google account.
  • How do I change the language? Settings › “🌐 Language”: Türkçe, English or Deutsch.
